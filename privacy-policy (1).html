Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

In short

  • We collect only what the app needs to run: your account details, the content you post, and basic device information.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • The app shows ads provided by Google, which may use your device’s advertising ID.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time.
  • ViralStar is for people aged 13 and over.

01About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how ViralStar (“ViralStar”, “we”, “us”), a short-video app developed by Awais Ahmad in Pakistan, collects, uses and protects your information. By using the app you agree to this policy.

02Information we collect

Account information. When you sign in with Google or an email address, we receive your name, email address and profile photo, along with the username, bio and region you choose in the app.

Content you share. Videos, photos, captions, comments, likes, follows and messages that you post or send in the app.

Camera, microphone and storage. The app asks for these permissions only when you record, upload or save a video. We do not use them in the background.

Device and usage information. General information such as device type, language, region and how the app is used. For advertising, Google may also use your device’s advertising ID.

03How we use information

  • To create and manage your account and display your profile and content.
  • To let you watch, post, like, comment and follow.
  • To show ads that help keep the app free.
  • To keep the community safe by reviewing reports and removing harmful content.
  • To fix problems and improve the app.

04Advertising

ViralStar shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Google may use your device’s advertising ID and similar information to deliver and measure ads. You can reset your advertising ID or turn off ad personalisation in your Android settings. Learn more at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

05Service providers

We use trusted third-party services, including Google services and cloud providers, to run sign-in, store data, host media and show ads. They process information on our behalf and under their own privacy policies. We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the safety of users and the app.

06What others can see

Your videos, photos, captions, comments, username, profile photo and bio may be visible to other users, depending on your privacy settings in the app. Please do not post personal information you do not want others to see.

07Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your information while your account is active. You can ask us to delete your account and the data linked to it at any time by following the steps on our Delete account page. Limited information may remain for a short time in backups or where the law requires it.

08Your choices

  • Update your profile and privacy settings inside the app.
  • Turn off camera, microphone or storage access in your phone settings.
  • Control ad personalisation in your Android settings.
  • Contact us to request access to, or deletion of, your data.

09Children

ViralStar is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child under 13 is using the app, please contact us and we will remove the account.

10Security

We use reputable providers and industry-standard protections to help keep your information safe. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version. Continuing to use ViralStar after an update means you accept the changes.

ViralStar Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

ViralStar ("the app", "we", "us") is a short-video app developed by Awais Ahmad (Swabi, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Pakistan). This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and what choices you have. By using ViralStar you agree to this policy.

Contact: awaisahmad16203@gmail.com
App package: com.viralstar.app

1. Information we collect

Account information. When you sign in with Google or create an account with an email address, we receive your name, email address and profile photo (from Google), and the username, display name, bio and country/region you set in the app.

Content you create. Videos, photos, captions, comments, likes, saves, reposts, follows, and messages that you post or send inside the app.

Camera, microphone and files. The app asks for camera and microphone permission only when you record or upload a video or photo. We do not access your camera or microphone in the background. On some Android versions the app may ask for storage permission so you can save videos to your Downloads folder.

Device and usage information. Your device language and region (used to show you the right country/region), and technical information such as device type, Android version and app interactions. Google AdMob also uses your device's Advertising ID to show ads (see section 4).

Local data on your device. Settings, screen-time limits, offline downloads and some app data are stored on your device.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and show your profile and content.
  • To let you post, watch, like, comment, follow and share videos.
  • To personalise your region and feed.
  • To show ads and keep the app free (Google AdMob).
  • To keep the app safe: handling reports, removing harmful content, and preventing abuse.
  • To fix bugs and improve the app.

4. Advertising

ViralStar shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's Advertising ID and similar device information to show ads and measure their performance. You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads). You can learn more at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

5. What is public

Videos, photos, captions, comments, your username, display name, profile photo and bio may be visible to other users depending on your privacy settings in the app. Please do not post personal information you do not want others to see.

6. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers listed above, when required by law, or to protect the safety and rights of users and the app.

7. Data retention and deletion

We keep your account information and content for as long as your account exists. You can ask us to delete your account and the data linked to it at any time. See the instructions at Delete your account. Some information may be kept for a short time in backups or where the law requires it.

8. Children

ViralStar is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your choices

  • Edit your profile and privacy settings in the app.
  • Withdraw camera, microphone or storage permission in your Android settings.
  • Control ad personalisation in your Android settings.
  • Request access to or deletion of your data by emailing us.

10. Security

We use Google Firebase and other reputable providers that protect data in transit using encryption. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed. Continuing to use the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

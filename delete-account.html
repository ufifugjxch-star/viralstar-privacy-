Delete your ViralStar account

App: ViralStar (com.viralstar.app) · Developer: Awais Ahmad

You can ask us to permanently delete your ViralStar account and the data linked to it at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to awaisahmad16203@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject Delete my ViralStar account.
  3. Send it from the same email address you use to sign in to ViralStar, and include your ViralStar username, so we can confirm the account is yours.

We will confirm by email and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile (name, username, bio, profile photo, country/region)
  • Your videos and photos, captions, comments, likes, saves, reposts and follows
  • Your sign-in record in Firebase Authentication

What may be kept

Copies in backups may remain for a short period before they are overwritten. We may also keep limited information where the law requires it or to prevent abuse and fraud.
